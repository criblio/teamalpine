# teamalpine

This is how the Cribl Security team shares R&D, threat intel, and related research.

## Purpose

teamalpine is the outlet for Cribl Security's R&D and threat intel output, covering:

**R&D** — research projects, proof-of-concepts, tooling experiments, and technical write-ups

**Threat intel** — indicators of compromise, threat actor tracking, vulnerability analysis, and industry advisories

**Notes and findings** — research summaries and reference material the team wants to share
Scope

Content here is meant to be shared — no sensitive or restricted data is posted to this repository.

## Contributing
Open a PR for new research, intel writeups, or tooling additions
Prefer dated folders or filenames for time-sensitive intel so context isn't lost over time
Contact

Questions: reach out to the Cribl Security team on our community slack or security@cribl.io
